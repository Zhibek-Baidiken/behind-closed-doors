01 / THE PROBLEMWHAT REMAINS INVISIBLE?

Protection on paper.
Protection in practice?

Domestic violence is not only a private matter. It is a question of access to institutions, legal protection, and meaningful support.

Kazakhstan's regions differ in geography, population, infrastructure, and access to services. This project asks whether women living in different places have comparable opportunities to obtain protection and assistance.

Expressions such as “Бьет — значит любит”, “Ұят болады”, and “Ыдыс-аяқ сылдырламай, үй болмайды” can open a discussion about social expectations. They do not, by themselves, establish how common particular attitudes are.

Recorded cases do not equal actual prevalence.

Higher counts may reflect population size, reporting, classification, or other factors. Do not use these values to rank regions by safety. The 2017 and 2025 totals in the draft also differ from the workbook's national totals because of omitted or separately categorized records.

04 / THE AI-ASSISTED SOLUTIONPROPOSAL · NOT A LIVE SUPPORT SERVICE

From fragmented data
to visible gaps.

A proposed public-information platform could help organize verified, non-personal data about domestic violence support infrastructure across regions. This website is a research prototype, not an operational AI service.

01

Collect

Gather official regional statistics and verified public information about services.

02

Organize

Use AI, where justified, to extract structured information from multilingual public documents.

03

Verify

Require human review of contact details, coverage, and AI-produced summaries.

04

Explain

Show possible gaps, uncertainties, and limitations without ranking regions as safe or unsafe.

What would it cost?

Environmental

Electricity, computing, and water footprint; compare with a static alternative.

Economic

Hosting, AI usage, data verification, security, and maintenance.

Labor

Human fact-checking, moderation, updates, and technical maintenance.

Governance

Control, accountability, surveillance risks, and applicable Kazakhstani law.

Data ethics

No survivor testimonies or identifying data required for the proposed analytical tool.

PROVISIONAL VERDICT

Only if AI adds value beyond a conventional directory.

A simpler, human-maintained public database may be safer and less expensive. A responsible proposal must test that alternative and document two comparable real-world AI case studies before recommending deployment.
